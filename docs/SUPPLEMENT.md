# Revision-backed supplement

The 24 revisions in `scripts/supplement.mjs` add a small cross-section of missing facts from MapleClassic Wiki. All derived facts are adapted from **MapleClassic Wiki contributors** under **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**. Each source entry in `data/supplement.json` carries the credited page, permanent revision link, revision timestamp, retrieval time and source build. The application retains these on supplemental facts and edges. Generated data may be shared and adapted under the same noncommercial license. [Reuse policy](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/MapleWiki:About#Reusing_the_wiki) · [License](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)

The online refresh requests named revision IDs in one MediaWiki query, checks the response set, and projects a small selection of typed fields and relationships. Wikitext, media, descriptions and player names are discarded. A normal offline import uses the checked-in projection. A new upstream revision requires reviewing and changing the explicit allowlist. Differences in extraction fail the import for the guarded recipe, price, quantity and compatibility inputs. [Import procedure](IMPORT.md)

**Scope.** Two quests have quantified inputs, required kills and deterministic rewards. Four store offers have historical unit prices. Two material recipes specify quantities, cost and crafting EXP. Selected items have numeric IDs, NPC resale values, recovery or upgrade properties. Cash pets and their Red Ribbon compatibility are identified from the item's explicit list. Scroll compatibility with Work Gloves is a typed derivation from the documented target category and a same-version item of that category. Its edge links the target evidence separately. [Quest 10211](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/Special:PermanentLink/89507) · [Quest 10212](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/Special:PermanentLink/89506) · [Shop](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/Special:PermanentLink/88324) · [Recipes](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/Special:PermanentLink/91652) · [Pet equipment](https://mapleclassic.wiki/wiki/Special:PermanentLink/90913)

**Versioning.** The supplement joins exact canonical source page and entity kind, preserving numeric source IDs as separate identifiers. Wiki 1.0.0, 0.9.0 and pre-launch assertions retain their own version even when attached to an existing entity. A version-specific price does not replace a base shop inventory assertion. A missing match for a required relationship aborts the import. New items require a source infobox ID. Recipe identity includes the profession ID as well as the local recipe ID. Price units are mesos per item.

The two recipe rows and four priced offers demonstrate the import without reproducing full upstream tables. Coverage includes the complete combined import, including entities which have no supplementary facts. [Coverage](DATA_COVERAGE.md)
